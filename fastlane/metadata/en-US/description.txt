Plan your soccer matchday on iPhone or iPad. Explore Wembley Stadium, Olympiastadion Berlin, and Stade de France with transit tips, stadium history, and orientation sketches. Use the sketch to get your bearings and check your ticket for your gate, section, row, and seat.

Add a home match by entering both teams, the stadium, and the local kickoff time. Choose how early you want to arrive and see your planned arrival time. Browse upcoming and past matches or filter your plans by date. You enter the matches yourself.

Use a reusable packing checklist and add your own items. Keep travel notes, matchday rituals, and fan song lyrics in a searchable journal. The stadium guide, match plans, checklist, and journal work offline.

Optional features include stadium weather, local notifications, Apple Calendar and Reminders exports, widgets, and a kickoff Live Activity on supported devices.
